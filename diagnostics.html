Diagnostic and Procedure Services

Diagnostic centre

An on-site laboratory providing selected haematology, biochemistry and serology tests, cardiac tests, and selected gastrointestinal and gynaecological procedures by appointment. Scans — 1.5 T MRI, CT, digital X-ray and ultrasound — have their own page.

Laboratory on site

Selected routine tests are processed on-site. Some tests may be referred to an external accredited laboratory. Available tests vary — contact the laboratory to confirm a specific investigation.

Cardiac tests

ECG and echocardiography may be available by appointment or clinical priority. Confirm current timings. Results are reviewed according to the current clinical and cardiology schedule; turnaround times vary.

Emergency laboratory

Selected urgent laboratory tests may be available for Emergency Department patients 24/7. Availability depends on the requested test and current operational capacity.

Tests and procedures should be selected by the treating clinician according to individual medical needs. This page is general information, not a basis for choosing your own investigation.
Tests Available

Diagnostic facilities

Diagnostic tests support clinical assessment. Availability and result times vary by test.

ECG

An electrocardiogram may be requested during clinical assessment, and is performed when requested by the treating clinician. New or severe chest pain requires immediate emergency evaluation — call 94875 76493.

Echocardiography (ECHO)

Echocardiography may be used to assess heart structure and function when clinically indicated. Studies are interpreted by an appropriately qualified clinician according to the current schedule.

Laboratory

Selected clinical laboratory investigations, including haematology, biochemistry and serology. Preoperative investigations are selected according to the planned procedure and clinical assessment.

Endoscopy and Related Procedures

Procedures by appointment

Selected diagnostic endoscopic procedures are available by specialist recommendation. Procedures are available by prior specialist assessment and confirmed appointment, and the procedure location depends on the examination and required level of monitoring.

Gastrointestinal endoscopy

Upper gastrointestinal endoscopy

Examination of the upper digestive tract when recommended by a clinician. Preparation and sedation requirements vary.

Colonoscopy

Examination of the colon when clinically recommended. Bowel preparation and medication instructions must be followed exactly.

Gynaecological diagnostic procedures

Colposcopy

Examination of the cervix following gynaecological assessment.

Hysteroscopy

Diagnostic or operative hysteroscopy may be available following gynaecological assessment.

Laparoscopic surgery is listed with surgical services

Laparoscopy is surgery rather than a diagnostic-centre test. Selected laparoscopic procedures may be performed after surgical assessment, informed consent and preoperative evaluation — see General Surgery and the Obstetrics & Gynaecology department.

Before You Come

Preparing for your test or procedure

Preparation varies by test or procedure. Follow the written instructions provided by the clinical team.

  • Bring the clinician's test request or referral and relevant previous reports.
  • Bring relevant previous reports, prescriptions and discharge summaries.
  • Confirm fasting and medication instructions with the laboratory or treating clinician.
  • Do not stop or change regular medicines unless your treating clinician or procedure team instructs you to do so.
  • Tell the clinician about anticoagulants, antiplatelet medicines and all regular medication. Do not stop them unless instructed.
  • Follow the written bowel-preparation instructions. Contact the procedure team if you cannot complete the preparation or become unwell.
  • If you have diabetes, ask the clinical team for specific fasting and medicine instructions.
  • Tell the team about allergies, previous sedation or anaesthesia reactions and relevant medical conditions.
  • Tell the clinical team if you are pregnant or may be pregnant.
  • Bring applicable insurance or scheme documents. Coverage remains subject to eligibility and authorization.

On the Day

Sedation, consent and aftercare

If sedation is planned

Arrange a responsible adult escort and follow the team's instructions about driving, work and decision-making afterward.

Consent

Before the procedure, the clinician will discuss its purpose, material risks, alternatives and expected recovery.

Aftercare

Aftercare and emergency-warning instructions will be provided before discharge. Contact the hospital immediately if advised warning symptoms occur.

Afterwards

Reports and results

When your report is ready

Report times vary by test. Ask for the expected collection time when the sample or test is registered. If a biopsy is taken, the pathology result will have a separate reporting time.

How reports are released

Reports are released through approved collection or digital channels after identity verification. Test records and reports are handled according to the hospital's privacy policy.

Urgent results

Critical results are communicated according to the hospital's clinical escalation policy. Your treating clinician will discuss the result with you.
