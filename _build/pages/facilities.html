Infrastructure

Hospital facilities

Selected emergency, critical-care, surgical, diagnostic and rehabilitation facilities are available on-site, including three operating theatres with access to C-Arm imaging for eligible procedures, and MRI, CT, X-ray and selected ultrasound services.

Hospital Facilities

Facilities that support coordinated care

Having selected services on-site may support coordination. Referral or transfer is arranged when required.

Facility access is subject to clinical need, staffing, capacity, maintenance and current operational availability. Clinical and restricted areas are accessible only to authorized patients, attendants and staff.

Ambulance

Emergency transport and selected inter-facility transfers may be available. Call 99438 27233 to request an ambulance — dispatch is subject to availability, location and operational conditions.

Intensive Care Unit

Critical-care monitoring may be available for patients meeting admission criteria, subject to bed and staffing availability. The ICU supports selected postoperative and medically unstable patients; transfer may be required for care beyond available capabilities.

Emergency Department

The Emergency Department operates 24/7 with patients prioritized according to clinical urgency. Transfer to available theatre or ICU facilities is arranged when clinically indicated.

Surgical Block

Three operating theatres with access to C-Arm imaging

The surgical block supports selected procedures across multiple specialties — orthopaedic fracture work, general and laparoscopic surgery, urological procedures and neurosurgery.

  • C-Arm imaging may provide intraoperative guidance for selected procedures
  • Selected gastrointestinal endoscopy, gynaecological procedures and laparoscopic surgery are available by specialist assessment
  • Emergency surgical transfer is coordinated according to clinical priority and theatre availability
  • Postoperative monitoring is provided in the recovery area, ward or ICU according to clinical need
  • Blood-bank support is subject to clinical need, compatibility, stock and regulatory requirements

Practical Information

Visiting the hospital

Reception on 04329 222530 can confirm the current details for the points below before you travel.

Accessibility

Ask reception about the accessible entrance, lift access, wheelchair assistance and help reaching consultation rooms. Tell reception when booking if the patient needs transfer support or language assistance.

Visitors and attendants

Visiting hours and the number of attendants allowed differ between the wards and the intensive care unit. Check with the ward nursing station on arrival.

Parking, waiting and amenities

Ask reception about parking, waiting areas, drinking water, food and restroom facilities when you plan your visit.

Safety and building services

The hospital maintains applicable infection-control, sterilization, fire-safety and backup-power procedures. Facility information last reviewed: August 2026 — call reception to confirm current hours, capacity and access.

Planning an admission?

Get admission and insurance information

For planned admissions, the team can provide an estimate based on the proposed care, explain available room categories subject to occupancy, and list the documents required. Actual charges may change according to clinical needs. Bring identification, referral records and applicable insurance or scheme documents — exact requirements depend on the admission type.

Emergency assessment is prioritized; administrative and insurance processes follow according to hospital policy.
