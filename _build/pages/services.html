What We Treat

Medical services

From accident rescue and intensive care to planned surgery, dialysis, cancer treatment and rehabilitation — the full range of services offered at Ariyalur Golden Hospital.

Round the Clock

Emergency & critical care

Our casualty department never closes. Imaging, theatre, blood bank and ICU are all in the same building, so treatment starts within minutes of arrival.

Emergency Care

24×7 accident and casualty department with immediate specialist referral, ambulance dispatch and resuscitation facilities.

  • Open all days, all hours
  • Ambulance on call
  • Direct theatre access

Trauma Care

Road traffic accident and injury management with on-site CT imaging, orthopaedic and neurosurgical support.

  • Free NK48 accident cover
  • CT scan on arrival
  • Fracture & head injury care

Intensive Care Unit

Monitored critical care beds for post-operative patients, cardiac emergencies, poisoning and severe infection.

  • 24-hour monitoring
  • Ventilatory support
  • Consultant-led care

Speciality Treatment

Surgical and speciality services

Every procedure below is performed within the hospital, supported by three C-Arm equipped theatres, in-house imaging and a blood bank.

Cardiology

ECG, ECHO and emergency management of heart attack, with interventional cardiology consultation on scheduled days.

Neurology

Brain, nerve and spinal cord treatment including neurosurgical procedures, supported by MRI and CT imaging.

Orthopaedics

Fracture treatment and fixation for accident injuries, with C-Arm guided theatre work and early physiotherapy.

Joint Replacement

Planned joint replacement surgery with pre-operative assessment, in-house imaging and structured rehabilitation.

Arthroscopy

Keyhole joint surgery for knee and shoulder problems, offering smaller incisions and quicker return to activity.

Spine Surgery

Spinal and spinal cord surgery for trauma and degenerative conditions, performed by the neurosurgical team.

General Surgery

Routine and emergency general surgical procedures with post-operative ICU support where required.

Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive abdominal surgery through small incisions, reducing pain and shortening hospital stay.

Kidney Stone Surgery

Surgical treatment for urinary stones and obstruction, performed by visiting M.Ch. urologists.

Prostate Surgery

Assessment and surgical management of prostate enlargement and related urinary symptoms.

Dialysis

Maintenance haemodialysis in a dedicated unit, available under government insurance schemes. Direct line: 84899 26941.

Oncology

Medical oncology consultation, treatment planning and follow-up on the 2nd and 4th Wednesday of each month.

Pulmonology

Treatment for asthma, COPD, chest infection and breathing difficulty, with chest imaging on site.

Poison Treatment

Emergency toxicology and poison management available 24 hours a day in the casualty department.

Maternity Care

24-hour delivery services with obstetric and gynaecological surgery. Booking line: 84899 26947.

Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation after surgery, fracture, stroke and prolonged illness, started during the hospital stay.
