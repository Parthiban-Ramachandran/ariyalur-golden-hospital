How It Works

Using your cover at our hospital

Bringing the required documents may help avoid delays during eligibility and pre-authorisation checks.

Documents to carry

Requirements vary by payer and admission type — the list below is indicative.

  • Your insurance e-card or scheme card, policy or beneficiary number, and employee ID where applicable
  • An accepted photo ID and the scheme- or insurer-required beneficiary document
  • Ration card or family card, only when required by the applicable government scheme
  • Depending on the case, referral notes, previous medical records, investigation reports, planned-procedure advice and an estimate
  • Any payer-issued authorisation documents

The process

  • For planned admission, share insurance details before admission. During an emergency, provide them as soon as reasonably possible
  • Our insurance desk performs a preliminary document check and submits a pre-authorisation request to the insurer, TPA or scheme
  • Emergency patients are assessed and managed according to clinical priority. Insurance documentation is processed as soon as practical
  • Approved eligible expenses are submitted for cashless settlement. Patients remain responsible for co-payments, deductibles, excluded items and any amount not approved by the payer

Protect your personal information

Do not upload or send identity cards, policy documents or medical records unless a hospital representative provides an approved secure method.

What approval does and does not mean

Pre-authorisation does not guarantee full claim settlement. Final approval depends on policy terms, treatment records, exclusions and the payer's assessment. Empanelment status, covered procedures and approval limits are decided by the insurer or the scheme, not by the hospital.

Costs that may not be covered

Coverage may be affected by waiting periods, room-category limits, sub-limits, co-payments, deductibles, exclusions and non-payable items.

If approval is declined or delayed

If cashless approval is declined or incomplete, payment may be required according to hospital policy. You may request claim documents for possible reimbursement from your insurer. Response times vary by insurer, TPA, scheme, treatment and document completeness — the hospital cannot guarantee an approval time.
