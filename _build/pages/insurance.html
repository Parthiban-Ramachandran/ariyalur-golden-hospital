How It Works

Using your cover at our hospital

Bring the right documents on the first visit and the approval process moves much faster.

Documents to carry

  • Insurance card, scheme card or policy number
  • Government photo identification (Aadhaar, voter ID or similar)
  • Ration card, where the scheme requires it
  • Referral letter or previous treatment records, if any
  • For planned surgery — the consultant's advice note and estimate

The process

  • Register at the front desk and declare your scheme or policy at admission
  • Our insurance desk verifies eligibility and raises the pre-authorisation request
  • Treatment begins — in an emergency, care is never delayed for paperwork
  • On discharge the claim is settled directly with the insurer or scheme wherever approved
