Before You Come

Preparing for your test

A few simple steps make your visit faster and your results more reliable.

  • Bring your prescription or referral letter from the consultant.
  • Carry previous scan films, reports and discharge summaries if you have them.
  • Ask the front desk whether fasting is required for your blood test or scan.
  • Inform the technologist if you have a pacemaker, metal implant or are pregnant before an MRI.
  • Bring your insurance or scheme card if the test is to be billed under a policy.
