Before You Come

Preparing for your test

A few simple steps make your visit faster and your results more reliable.

  • Bring your prescription or referral letter from the consultant.
  • Carry previous reports and discharge summaries if you have them.
  • Ask the front desk whether fasting is required for your blood test.
  • Follow the bowel preparation instructions given to you before a colonoscopy.
  • Tell the doctor about blood thinners or other regular medication before a scope procedure.
  • Bring your insurance or scheme card if the test is to be billed under a policy.
