About the Hospital

A multispecialty hospital for the Ariyalur region

Ariyalur Golden Hospital brings emergency medicine, surgery, critical care and imaging together on one site. The hospital provides a range of services locally and arranges referral when care beyond its available facilities is required.

The accident and emergency department operates 24/7, supported by an intensive care unit, an in-house blood bank and ambulance services. Consultations are available in selected specialties on scheduled days — including cardiology, neurology and neurosurgery, pulmonology, urology, nephrology, medical oncology, radiology, general medicine, orthopaedics, general surgery, physiotherapy and plastic surgery. Please call to confirm the current doctor schedule.

  • Operating theatres with C-Arm imaging
  • Intensive care unit
  • MRI 1.5 T, CT scan and digital X-ray
  • Dialysis unit
  • Endoscopy and laparoscopy
  • Maternity and delivery care

Facility availability varies. Please call before travelling for a specific service.

Services & Facilities

Services and facilities available at the hospital

Availability of individual services varies. Please call before travelling for a specific service.

24/7 Emergency Department

Casualty, trauma care and ambulance services. Emergency patients are assessed and transferred to available critical-care or surgical facilities according to clinical need.

Specialist Consultations

Resident and visiting consultants in selected specialties on scheduled days. Call to confirm the current doctor schedule.

Diagnostics & Imaging

Selected diagnostic and imaging services are available on-site, including MRI 1.5 T, CT, digital X-ray, ECG and ECHO. Please confirm scheduling and report timelines.

Blood Bank & Dialysis

An in-house blood bank and a dialysis unit. Dialysis may be covered under eligible government health schemes — contact the insurance desk to confirm eligibility.

Insurance & Schemes

Cashless treatment may be available, subject to insurer or scheme approval and current hospital empanelment. Please check with the insurance desk before admission.

Ambulance

Call 99438 27233 to request an ambulance for emergency pick-up or inter-facility transfer. Dispatch is subject to availability and location.

Patient Information

Planning your visit

Practical details for outpatient visits, admissions and report collection. Timings are confirmed by reception on 04329 222530.

Outpatient consultations

OP consultations are by token. Call advance token booking on 94875 76493 for OP appointments, 84899 26947 for maternity and surgical treatments, or reception for the current OP hours and doctor schedule.

Documents to bring

Bring a photo ID, any previous prescriptions, discharge summaries, scan films and reports, and your insurance or government-scheme card if you intend to use one.

Reports & records

Laboratory and imaging report timelines vary by test. Ask the diagnostic desk when your report will be ready and how it can be collected.

Visitors

Visiting hours and the number of attendants allowed differ between the wards and the intensive care unit. Please check with the ward nursing station on arrival.

Insurance desk

Cashless treatment and government-scheme cover are subject to approval, eligibility and current hospital empanelment. Contact the insurance desk before admission wherever possible.

Accessibility

Wheelchair assistance and help reaching the consultation rooms can be requested at the front desk. Ambulance transport is available on 99438 27233, subject to availability.
