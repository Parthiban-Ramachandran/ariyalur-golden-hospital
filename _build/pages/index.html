About the Hospital

A complete multi-speciality hospital built for the Ariyalur region

Ariyalur Golden Hospital brings emergency medicine, surgery, critical care and advanced imaging together under one roof — so that patients no longer need to travel long distances for quality treatment.

Our accident and emergency department works round the clock, supported by a 24-hour intensive care unit, an in-house blood bank and ambulance services. Specialist consultants practise across cardiology, neurology and neurosurgery, pulmonology, urology, nephrology, medical oncology, radiology, general medicine, orthopaedics, general surgery, physiotherapy and plastic surgery.

  • Three modern operation theatres with C-Arm
  • 24-hour ICU and critical care
  • MRI 1.5 Tesla, CT scan, digital X-ray
  • Dialysis unit with scheme coverage
  • Full endoscopy & laparoscopy suite
  • 24-hour maternity and delivery care
