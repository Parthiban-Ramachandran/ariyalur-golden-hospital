Simple Process

How your check-up day works

1 · Book your slot

Call the front desk or send an online request. We will tell you whether fasting is required.

2 · Samples & tests

Blood samples, ECG and imaging are completed in sequence during a single morning visit.

3 · Reports

Your consolidated report is prepared with all investigations in one document.

4 · Doctor review

A physician explains the findings, flags anything abnormal and advises on follow-up.

Why It Matters

Most serious illness gives warning long before symptoms

Diabetes, high blood pressure, kidney disease and heart disease can progress silently for years. A yearly check-up turns a future emergency into a manageable prescription.

  • Detect diabetes and hypertension before organ damage begins
  • Assess kidney function early — especially important if dialysis runs in the family
  • Identify cardiac risk with ECG and lipid screening
  • Establish a baseline record for future comparison
  • All investigations and the review consultation in one visit
