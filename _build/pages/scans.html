Before You Come

Preparing for your scan

A few simple steps make your visit faster and your images more reliable.

  • Bring your prescription or referral letter from the consultant.
  • Carry previous scan films, reports and discharge summaries if you have them.
  • Ask the front desk whether fasting is required before your scan.
  • Inform the technologist if you have a pacemaker, metal implant or are pregnant before an MRI.
  • Wear loose clothing without metal fastenings and leave jewellery at home.
  • Bring your insurance or scheme card if the scan is to be billed under a policy.
