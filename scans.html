Safety & Screening

What to tell the imaging team

Tell staff about the points below before your examination. The imaging team assesses safety — please do not decide for yourself whether an implant or medicine is a problem.

Before an MRI

Tell staff about any implanted device, pacemaker, surgery involving metal, metal fragments, hearing implant, pregnancy or possible pregnancy. The MRI team will assess safety.

Tell the imaging team in advance if you have claustrophobia or difficulty lying still. Wear comfortable clothing and follow staff instructions about removing jewellery, watches, cards, hearing aids and other metallic or electronic items.

Before a CT or X-ray

CT uses X-rays. The referring clinician and imaging team consider the expected benefit and radiation exposure before the examination.

Tell the imaging team before any CT or X-ray if you are pregnant or may be pregnant.

If contrast may be used

Before a contrast scan, tell staff about kidney disease, previous contrast reactions, allergies and current medicines. Follow only the preparation instructions given for your examination.

If MRI contrast may be used, also tell staff about pregnancy or breastfeeding.

Children and extra assistance

For a child's scan, ask the imaging team about preparation, accompaniment and whether additional planning is required.

Tell reception when booking if the patient needs wheelchair assistance, transfer support or language assistance.

Before You Come

Preparing for your scan

Preparation varies by examination. Follow the instructions given by the imaging team when your appointment is confirmed.

  • Bring the clinician's imaging request or referral and any relevant previous records.
  • Bring relevant previous images, reports and discharge summaries for comparison.
  • Confirm fasting and medication instructions with the imaging team before your appointment.
  • Do not stop regular medicines unless your clinician or imaging team specifically instructs you to do so.
  • Wear loose clothing without metal fastenings, and follow staff instructions about items to remove.
  • Bring applicable insurance or scheme documents. Coverage remains subject to eligibility and authorization.
  • Call reception for the current fee and any contrast-related additional charges.

Afterwards

Reports and images

Collecting your report

Ask when your report will be ready and whether it will be collected in person or provided through an approved digital channel. Bring proof of identity when collecting.

Urgent findings

Urgent findings are communicated according to the hospital's clinical escalation policy. Your treating clinician will discuss the result with you.

Copies of your images

Confirm whether images are supplied digitally, on film or by another approved method, and ask about any charge, before you need them for a referral elsewhere.
