Infrastructure

Hospital facilities

Selected emergency, critical-care, surgical, diagnostic and rehabilitation facilities are available on-site, including two operating theatres with access to C-Arm imaging for eligible procedures, and MRI, CT, X-ray and selected ultrasound services. The ICU, blood bank, dialysis unit, operating theatres and CT operate 24/7.

Hospital Facilities

Facilities that support coordinated care

Having selected services on-site may support coordination. Referral or transfer is arranged when required.

Facility access is subject to clinical need, staffing, capacity, maintenance and current operational availability. Clinical and restricted areas are accessible only to authorized patients, attendants and staff.

Ambulance

Emergency transport and selected inter-facility transfers may be available. Call 94875 76493 to request an ambulance — dispatch is subject to availability, location and operational conditions.

24/7 request line

Intensive Care Unit

The ICU is staffed 24/7 for patients meeting admission criteria, subject to bed availability. It supports selected postoperative and medically unstable patients; transfer may be required for care beyond available capabilities.

24/7Admission by clinical criteria

Blood Bank

The on-site blood bank operates 24/7 and supports eligible patient-care needs, subject to stock, compatibility and regulatory requirements. Contact the hospital for available components.

24/7On-site

Dialysis Unit

The dialysis unit runs 24/7. Maintenance haemodialysis is provided by confirmed appointment and clinical assessment, with urgent sessions arranged when clinically indicated. Eligible government-scheme coverage may be available, subject to current empanelment and approval.

24/7Dialysis enquiries: 84899 26941

Operating Theatres

Two operation theatres, available 24/7 for emergency surgery — an orthopaedic theatre with C-Arm imaging and a general theatre with laparoscopic facility. Selected orthopaedic, general, urological and neurosurgical procedures may be performed following specialist assessment; planned procedures are scheduled with the surgical team.

24/72 theatres

C-Arm imaging

C-Arm imaging may provide intraoperative guidance for selected procedures, including fracture fixation. Availability for a specific procedure is confirmed by the surgical team.

Theatre imaging

Philips Achieva 1.5T MRI

On-site Philips Achieva 1.5T MRI for selected examinations of the brain, spine, joints and soft tissue, requested by a clinician.

Surgical Block

Two operating theatres with access to C-Arm imaging

The surgical block supports selected procedures across multiple specialties — orthopaedic fracture work, general and laparoscopic surgery, urological procedures and neurosurgery.

  • An orthopaedic operation theatre with C-Arm imaging
  • A general operation theatre with laparoscopic facility
  • C-Arm imaging may provide intraoperative guidance for selected procedures
  • Selected gastrointestinal endoscopy, gynaecological procedures and laparoscopic surgery are available by specialist assessment
  • Theatres are available 24/7, with emergency surgery coordinated according to clinical priority
  • Postoperative monitoring is provided in the recovery area, ward or the 24/7 ICU according to clinical need
  • 24/7 blood-bank support, subject to clinical need, compatibility, stock and regulatory requirements

Practical Information

Visiting the hospital

Reception on 04329 222530 can confirm the current details for the points below before you travel.

Accessibility

Ask reception about the accessible entrance, lift access, wheelchair assistance and help reaching consultation rooms. Tell reception when booking if the patient needs transfer support or language assistance.

Visitors and attendants

Visiting hours and the number of attendants allowed differ between the wards and the intensive care unit. Check with the ward nursing station on arrival.

Parking, waiting and amenities

Ask reception about parking, waiting areas, drinking water, food and restroom facilities when you plan your visit.

Safety and building services

The hospital maintains applicable infection-control, sterilization, fire-safety and backup-power procedures. Call reception to confirm current hours, capacity and access.

Planning an admission?

Get admission and insurance information

For planned admissions, the team can provide an estimate based on the proposed care, explain available room categories subject to occupancy, and list the documents required. Actual charges may change according to clinical needs. Bring identification, referral records and applicable insurance or scheme documents — exact requirements depend on the admission type.

Emergency assessment is prioritized; administrative and insurance processes follow according to hospital policy.
