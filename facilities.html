Surgical Block

Three ultra-modern operation theatres with C-Arm

Our surgical block is built to keep multiple specialities running in parallel — orthopaedic fracture work, general and laparoscopic surgery, urological procedures and neurosurgery.

  • C-Arm image intensifier for intra-operative guidance
  • Endoscopy, colonoscopy, colposcopy, hysteroscopy and laparoscopy
  • Direct transfer route from the emergency ward
  • Post-operative recovery supported by the intensive care unit
  • In-house blood bank support for major surgery
