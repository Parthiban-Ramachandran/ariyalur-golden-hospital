Medical & website disclaimer

How to read the information published on this website, and what it does and does not represent.

Medical emergency? Do not use this website

If you or someone you know is experiencing a life-threatening medical emergency, call our 24×7 emergency line immediately or come directly to our Emergency Department at Periyar Nagar, Ariyalur. Never rely on website messages or online forms for urgent medical assistance.

1. General information only

The content provided on the Ariyalur Golden Hospital (PVT) Ltd. website (https://ariyalur-golden-hospital.vercel.app/) is for general informational and educational purposes only. It is designed to assist visitors in understanding our healthcare services, medical departments, diagnostic facilities and appointment scheduling.

2. No doctor–patient relationship

Browsing this website, submitting an appointment request, or contacting us through online forms does not establish a formal doctor–patient relationship. A formal doctor–patient relationship is established only upon an in-person or officially registered clinical consultation with one of our qualified medical professionals.

3. Not professional medical advice

The information on this website should not be used as a self-diagnosis tool or as a substitute for professional medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Always consult a qualified healthcare provider regarding any medical condition or treatment plan.

Never delay care because of something you read here

Do not disregard, avoid or delay obtaining medical advice from a qualified healthcare professional because of information published on this website.
