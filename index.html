About the Hospital

A complete multi-speciality hospital built for the Ariyalur region

Ariyalur Golden Hospital (PVT) Ltd. brings emergency medicine, surgery, critical care and advanced imaging together under one roof — so that patients no longer need to travel long distances for quality treatment.

Our accident and emergency department works round the clock, supported by a 24-hour intensive care unit, an in-house blood bank and ambulance services. Specialist consultants practise across cardiology, neurology and neurosurgery, pulmonology, urology, nephrology, medical oncology, radiology, general medicine, orthopaedics, general surgery, physiotherapy and plastic surgery.

  • Three modern operation theatres with C-Arm
  • 24-hour ICU and critical care
  • MRI 1.5 Tesla, CT scan, digital X-ray
  • Dialysis unit with scheme coverage
  • Full endoscopy & laparoscopy suite
  • 24-hour maternity and delivery care

Why Choose Us

Care that is prompt, precise and within reach

Every service is designed around one idea — that critical treatment should never be delayed by distance or paperwork.

24×7 Emergency Response

Casualty, trauma care and ambulance services available every hour of the year, with immediate access to ICU and theatre.

Specialist Consultants

Visiting and resident consultants across 15+ specialities, including interventional cardiology and neurosurgery.

Advanced Imaging In-House

MRI 1.5 Tesla, CT scan, digital X-ray, ECG and ECHO — reports without referral delays or outside travel.

Blood Bank & Dialysis

An in-house blood bank and a dedicated dialysis unit, with treatment available under government schemes.

Cashless Treatment

Empanelled under government schemes and leading private TPAs including Star Health, Medi Assist, MD India and VIDAL.

Ambulance on Call

Equipped ambulance service for emergency pick-up and inter-facility transfer, dispatched on a single call.
