Hospital Services

Medical services

Explore selected services available at Ariyalur Golden Hospital — emergency and trauma care, intensive care, planned surgery, dialysis, medical oncology consultation and treatment planning, and rehabilitation.

Services, investigations and treatment depend on clinical assessment, specialist availability and hospital capacity. If a required service is unavailable, the treating team will discuss referral or transfer options.
Frequently Used Services

Services

Consultant schedules vary by service; call reception before travelling.

Orthopaedics

Selected fracture, joint and spine procedures may be available following specialist assessment. Physiotherapy may begin when prescribed and clinically appropriate.

  • Trauma & fracture fixation
  • Joint replacement
  • Arthroscopy & spine surgery
Orthopaedics department

General Surgery

Planned and emergency surgical assessment is available, subject to clinical and operational capacity. Open and laparoscopic procedures, with post-operative critical care where required.

  • Emergency & planned surgery
  • Laparoscopic procedures
  • ICU backup on site
General surgery department

Laser Surgery

Laser-assisted procedures may be suitable for selected patients after surgical assessment. Admission and discharge timing depend on the procedure, recovery and individual clinical needs. Each procedure is performed by the responsible specialist.

  • Piles, fissure & fistula
  • Varicose vein treatment (confirm the treating specialist)
  • Day-care admission where clinically appropriate
Laser surgery department

Dialysis

The dialysis unit runs 24/7, with nephrology consultation and laboratory support on-site. Eligible government-scheme coverage may be available, subject to current empanelment and approval.

  • Maintenance haemodialysis, available 24/7
  • Scheme coverage subject to eligibility and authorization
  • Dialysis enquiries — not an emergency line. Call 84899 26941
Nephrology & dialysis department
24/7 Emergency Department

Emergency & critical care

The Emergency Department operates 24/7. Patients are assessed and prioritized according to clinical urgency. CT, operating theatres, the blood bank, dialysis and the ICU operate 24/7 on-site, subject to clinical criteria and current capacity.

Emergency Care

24/7 emergency and accident care with resuscitation facilities. Specialist consultation or referral is arranged according to clinical need and availability.

  • Open all days, all hours
  • Call the emergency line to request an ambulance; dispatch is subject to availability and location
  • Emergency surgery is arranged when clinically indicated and operationally available

Trauma Care

Road traffic accident and injury management. Trauma assessment may include imaging and specialist consultation according to clinical need and availability.

  • Eligible accident-care scheme coverage may be available, subject to current government rules and hospital empanelment
  • X-ray, CT or other imaging may be requested after clinical assessment
  • Assessment and initial management of fractures and head injuries, with referral or transfer when required

Intensive Care Unit

The ICU is staffed 24/7, providing critical-care monitoring and respiratory support for patients who meet clinical admission criteria.

  • Continuous 24/7 monitoring for admitted patients
  • Respiratory support where clinically indicated
  • Admission is subject to clinical criteria and bed availability

Speciality Treatment

Other surgical and speciality services

The following specialist consultations and selected procedures may be available at the hospital. The hospital has operating theatres with access to C-Arm imaging for eligible procedures, alongside on-site imaging and a blood bank.

Cardiology

ECG and echocardiography (ECHO). Emergency assessment and initial management of suspected heart attack are available; transfer may be required for procedures not available on-site. Visiting cardiology consultations are available on scheduled days.

Neurology

Neurology and visiting neurosurgery consultations are available for selected conditions; procedures and referral needs are determined after assessment. MRI and CT imaging are available on-site.

Joint Replacement

Planned joint replacement surgery with pre-operative assessment and on-site imaging. Post-operative rehabilitation is planned according to the patient's clinical needs.

Arthroscopy

Arthroscopy uses small incisions and may be recommended for selected joint conditions of the knee and shoulder.

Spine Surgery

Selected spine procedures may be considered after specialist assessment and confirmation of hospital availability.

Laparoscopic Surgery

For eligible patients, laparoscopic surgery may offer smaller incisions; risks, alternatives and recovery should be discussed with the surgeon.

Kidney Stone Surgery

Selected stone procedures may be available following urology assessment; confirm the current consultant schedule.

Prostate Surgery

Assessment and treatment planning for prostate-related urinary symptoms.

Oncology

Medical oncology consultation, treatment planning and follow-up. Usually scheduled on selected Wednesdays; call reception to confirm.

Pulmonology

Consultation for selected respiratory conditions, with chest imaging on site. Severe or sudden breathing difficulty requires emergency assessment.

Suspected Poisoning

Emergency assessment for suspected poisoning is available. Call immediately on 94875 76493 and do not induce vomiting unless directed by a qualified professional. Do not attempt home treatment. Bring the product container or label only if it is safe to do so.

Maternity Care

Labour, delivery and selected obstetric services are available; call the maternity team to confirm current coverage. Maternity & surgical treatment enquiries: 84899 26947 — not for emergencies; call to confirm hours.

Physiotherapy

Rehabilitation after surgery, fracture, stroke and prolonged illness. Inpatient physiotherapy may begin when prescribed and clinically appropriate.
