Cardiology

Consultations and initial assessment of cardiac symptoms

Consultation and investigation for chest pain, breathlessness, palpitations and known heart disease. Emergency assessment and initial management of suspected heart attack are available; patients may require transfer for procedures not available on-site. Visiting cardiology consultations are available on scheduled days — confirm whether any procedure requires referral.

Treatments
  • Emergency assessment and initial management of suspected heart attack
  • ECG evaluation
  • Echocardiography (ECHO)
  • Visiting cardiology consultation — procedures may require referral
  • Hypertension & cardiac risk assessment
