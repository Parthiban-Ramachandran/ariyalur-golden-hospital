Not for urgent symptoms

Health check-up packages are intended for preventive assessment, not urgent symptoms. Seek prompt medical care for new or severe symptoms — call the Emergency Department on 94875 76493.

The Process

How your check-up works

1 · Request an appointment

The laboratory or health-check team will provide test-specific fasting and medication instructions.

2 · Samples & tests

Included tests are scheduled according to availability. Some investigations may require a separate appointment.

3 · Reports

Available results are compiled or provided through the hospital's approved reporting process. Some reports may be issued separately.

4 · Clinician review

A clinician reviews available results and discusses whether follow-up or further assessment is needed.

Before You Come

Preparing for your check-up

Follow the exact instructions given by the health-check team when your appointment is confirmed.

Fasting and medicines

Follow the exact fasting instructions provided; ask whether plain water is permitted. Do not stop or change medicines unless instructed. People with diabetes should request specific fasting and medication advice.

What to bring

Bring identification, previous relevant reports, current medicine details and any requested samples or forms.

Before any imaging

Tell the health-check team if you are pregnant or may be pregnant before any imaging is arranged.

Why It Matters

Appropriate screening can identify selected health risks early

Some conditions can be identified before symptoms develop. Appropriate screening depends on age, history, risk factors and clinical guidance. Regular health review may help identify selected risk factors and support timely follow-up.

  • Screen for elevated blood glucose and blood pressure, which may be present without obvious symptoms
  • Kidney assessment may be particularly relevant for people with diabetes, hypertension, known kidney risk or a family history of kidney disease
  • Cardiovascular-risk assessment may include blood pressure, medical history and lipid testing. ECG is used when clinically appropriate
  • Results can support future comparison, but normal screening does not exclude all disease
  • Where possible, included tests are coordinated efficiently; report review may occur after all results are available

What screening can and cannot do

Screening cannot detect every condition. Results may occasionally require repeat testing or further assessment.

Afterwards

Results and follow-up

Abnormal or urgent findings

Urgent findings are communicated according to the hospital's clinical escalation policy. The reviewing clinician will explain what the results mean for you.

What is not included

Additional tests, specialist consultations and treatment are not included unless specifically stated and will be discussed before booking.

Report privacy

Results are released securely to the individual or an authorized recipient in accordance with the hospital's privacy policy.
