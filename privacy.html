Privacy policy

How we collect, store, process and protect the personal and medical information of our patients, caregivers and website visitors.

Effective date: 11 August 2026 Last updated: 11 August 2026

Ariyalur Golden Hospital (PVT) Ltd. (“Ariyalur Golden Hospital”, “Hospital”, “we”, “us” or “our”) respects the privacy of our patients, caregivers, visitors, job applicants and website users. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, share and protect personal information when you interact with us through our website, telephone, hospital registration, appointment enquiries, recruitment forms or healthcare services.

We handle personal information in accordance with applicable Indian laws, to the extent that their respective provisions are applicable and in force from time to time, including:

  • The Information Technology Act, 2000
  • The Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011
  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Rules, 2025

This policy sits alongside our hospital notices

This website Privacy Policy does not replace any additional consent form, patient-information notice or privacy information that may be provided during hospital registration, admission, treatment, insurance processing or other clinical services.

1. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with the Hospital, we may collect:

  • Identity and contact information — your name, age, date of birth, gender, residential address, mobile number, email address, emergency contact information and other identification information required for hospital administration.
  • Health and medical information — information relating to your health, symptoms, medical history, diagnosis, medications, allergies, treatment, procedures, prescriptions, physician notes, discharge information, laboratory results, MRI, CT, X-ray, ultrasound and other diagnostic reports.
  • Appointment and enquiry information — information provided when you contact us regarding an appointment, doctor consultation, scan, diagnostic service, health check-up, dialysis, maternity service or other hospital service.
  • Insurance and government-scheme information — insurance or TPA information, policy or beneficiary details and information required for eligible government healthcare schemes, including Innuyir Kappom – Nammai Kaakkum 48, where applicable.
  • Billing and payment information — billing records, payment details, invoices and information required for accounting, insurance, reimbursement or hospital administration.
  • Job-application information — when you apply for employment, we may collect your name, contact details, qualifications, employment history, professional or council registration information, experience, CV, certificates and other information submitted as part of your application.
  • Website and technical information — when you use our website, our hosting, security or technical systems may automatically generate limited information such as your IP address, browser type, device type, pages requested, date and time of access and similar technical logs.

Please keep applications free of patient information

Do not include patient information or unnecessary sensitive information in a job application.

2. How we collect information

We may receive personal information:

  • directly from you;
  • from a parent, guardian, caregiver or authorised representative;
  • from doctors, hospitals, laboratories or other healthcare providers involved in your care;
  • from insurers, TPAs or government healthcare programmes;
  • through website forms or employment applications;
  • during telephone, email or in-person enquiries; and
  • automatically through website infrastructure and technical logs.

3. How we use your information

We may use personal information where reasonably necessary to:

  • provide medical, diagnostic, surgical, emergency, outpatient and inpatient healthcare services;
  • register patients and maintain appropriate medical records;
  • arrange appointments, consultations and diagnostic services;
  • communicate appointment information, reports or service-related updates;
  • manage hospital admissions, discharge and follow-up;
  • process billing, insurance, cashless-treatment and government-scheme claims;
  • coordinate care between healthcare professionals;
  • respond to patient, caregiver and general enquiries;
  • manage recruitment and assess employment applications;
  • maintain the security and operation of our website and information systems;
  • conduct lawful hospital administration, quality-management and patient-safety activities; and
  • comply with legal, regulatory, court, public-health or governmental requirements.

We will not use personal information for an unrelated purpose unless permitted by applicable law or appropriate consent has been obtained where required.

4. Consent and communications

Where consent is required for particular processing, we will seek appropriate consent through the relevant form, interaction or hospital process.

Certain personal information may also need to be processed without separate consent where permitted or required by applicable law, including circumstances involving medical care, emergencies, legal obligations, insurance administration or regulatory requirements.

If you have consented to optional or promotional communications, you may ask us to stop those communications at any time.

Withdrawal of consent does not affect processing that occurred lawfully before withdrawal and may not require us to delete information that we must retain for medical, legal, regulatory, insurance, accounting or other lawful purposes.

5. How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal or medical information. Information may be shared only where reasonably necessary with:

  • Healthcare professionals — doctors, consultants, nurses, technicians, pharmacists and other personnel involved in your care.
  • Diagnostic and healthcare providers — laboratories, imaging providers, blood-bank services, referral hospitals or other healthcare providers where required for diagnosis, treatment or continuity of care.
  • Insurance companies, TPAs and government healthcare schemes — where information is necessary to verify eligibility, obtain pre-authorisation, process claims or administer benefits.
  • Technology and service providers — hosting providers, software providers, communication services, data-storage providers and other vendors assisting us with hospital or website operations, subject to appropriate confidentiality, contractual and security requirements where applicable.
  • Professional advisers — auditors, accountants, legal advisers or other professional advisers where reasonably required.
  • Government, regulatory and law-enforcement authorities — where disclosure is required or permitted by applicable law, legal process, court order or regulatory requirement.

We seek to limit any disclosure to the information reasonably required for its purpose.

6. Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purpose for which it was collected or as required by applicable medical, legal, regulatory, insurance, accounting, taxation, dispute-resolution or other requirements.

Different categories of medical records may be subject to different statutory or professional retention requirements.

Job-application information is retained only for as long as reasonably necessary for recruitment purposes or as otherwise permitted or required by law. If we wish to consider an applicant for future vacancies, information may be retained for that purpose where appropriate.

When information is no longer reasonably required and no legal or operational requirement requires continued retention, we may securely delete, anonymise or otherwise dispose of it.

7. Information security

We use reasonable administrative, physical and technical measures designed to protect personal information against unauthorised access, disclosure, alteration, loss, misuse or destruction. Depending on the system and information involved, these measures may include:

  • access controls and authentication;
  • limiting access according to staff responsibilities;
  • confidentiality requirements for employees and service providers;
  • system logging and monitoring;
  • backups and recovery procedures;
  • secure transmission or encryption where appropriate; and
  • appropriate security requirements for service providers handling information on our behalf.

No website, electronic transmission or information-storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. If we become aware of a personal-data security incident, we will assess and respond to it in accordance with applicable law and our internal procedures.

8. Your privacy choices and requests

Depending on applicable law, the nature of the information and the circumstances of the request, you may contact us to:

Access information

Ask whether we hold personal information relating to you and request appropriate information about its processing.

Correct or update information

Ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete personal information.

Request deletion

Ask us to delete information where it is no longer necessary and we are not legally or operationally required to retain it. Medical records, billing records and other regulated information may need to be retained even after a deletion request.

Withdraw consent

Withdraw consent for processing that depends upon your consent, subject to applicable legal and medical requirements.

Opt out of optional communications

Ask us to stop promotional or other non-essential communications.

Raise a grievance

Contact our Privacy / Grievance Contact if you believe your personal information has not been handled appropriately.

We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting on a privacy request.

As additional provisions of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and associated Rules become applicable, we will provide the rights, procedures and grievance mechanisms required by those provisions.

9. Children and minors

Ariyalur Golden Hospital provides healthcare services to patients of different ages, including children.

Where appropriate, personal information relating to a child may be provided by or handled through a parent, legal guardian or other authorised person.

The Hospital will process information relating to children in accordance with applicable healthcare and privacy requirements. Emergency medical care and legally required processing may be handled according to applicable law and clinical requirements.

10. Job applicants

Information submitted through our Careers page is used primarily to:

  • review your qualifications and suitability for a vacancy;
  • contact you about your application;
  • verify qualifications or professional registration where appropriate;
  • arrange interviews or assessments; and
  • comply with employment, regulatory or legal requirements.

Providing an application does not guarantee employment. Please submit only information relevant to your application and do not include any patient information in your CV, documents or application.

11. Cookies and website technologies

Our website and hosting infrastructure may use cookies or similar technologies that are necessary for website functionality, security or technical operation.

Technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type and page-access information may also be recorded through server logs.

Where non-essential analytics, advertising or other tracking technologies are introduced, we will provide any notice or choice required by applicable law. We do not intentionally use cookies for the purpose of storing medical records.

12. Third-party websites and services

Our website may contain links to third-party services such as maps, social-media pages, insurers or other external websites.

Those websites operate independently and may have their own privacy policies and data-processing practices. Ariyalur Golden Hospital is not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party websites that we do not control.

13. Processing by service providers outside India

Some technology or service providers may use infrastructure located outside India.

Where personal information is transferred or processed outside India, we will handle such processing subject to applicable Indian law and any restrictions or safeguards that apply from time to time.
