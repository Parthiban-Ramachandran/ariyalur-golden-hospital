Privacy policy

How we collect, store, process and protect the personal and medical information of our patients, caregivers and website visitors.

Effective date: July 2026 Last updated: July 2026

Ariyalur Golden Hospital (PVT) Ltd. (“We”, “Us” or “Our”) is dedicated to safeguarding the privacy and personal health data of our patients, caregivers, visitors and website users (“You” or “Your”). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, process and protect your personal and medical information in accordance with Indian privacy laws, including:

  • The Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA)
  • The Information Technology Act, 2000 (Section 43A)
  • The Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices and Procedures and Sensitive Personal Data or Information) Rules, 2011

On this page

  1. Information we collect
  2. Legal basis & purpose of processing
  3. Data sharing & disclosure
  4. Data security practices
  5. Your rights under DPDPA 2023

1. Information we collect

We collect the following types of information through online interactions, website forms, appointment bookings and hospital registration:

  • Identity & contact data — full name, gender, date of birth, age, residential address, mobile phone number, email address and emergency contact details.
  • Health & medical data — medical history, symptoms, diagnostic scan reports (MRI, CT, digital X-ray), laboratory reports, treatment records and physician notes.
  • Insurance & financial data — government scheme details (for example, Nammai Kaappom 48), Third-Party Administrator (TPA) card details, billing records and payment transactions.
  • Technical & website usage data — IP address, browser type, device identifiers and page interaction data collected via cookies.

2. Legal basis & purpose of data processing

We process your personal and health data strictly for lawful purposes, including:

  • Delivering clinical diagnostic, surgical, emergency and outpatient medical services.
  • Processing token bookings and confirming doctor appointment slots.
  • Managing hospital administrative tasks, billing and maintaining electronic health records.
  • Facilitating cashless insurance pre-authorisations and government scheme claims.
  • Sending SMS, WhatsApp or phone reminders for appointment schedules and health check-ups.
  • Complying with legal, statutory and public health reporting mandates.

3. Data sharing & disclosure

We value your privacy and do not sell or rent your personal information. Information is shared strictly on a need-to-know basis in the following circumstances:

  • Healthcare professionals — attending doctors, surgeons, nurses and radiologists involved in your diagnosis and care.
  • Diagnostic & clinical partners — in-house and partner diagnostic labs, imaging suites and blood bank management.
  • Insurance companies & TPAs — Star Health, Medi Assist, MD India, VIDAL and government bodies, for processing cashless hospitalisation claims.
  • Legal authorities — when required by applicable law, court orders or statutory regulations.

4. Data security practices

In compliance with Section 43A of the IT Act, 2000, we maintain reasonable physical, technical and administrative security measures, including:

  • Role-based access control, ensuring only authorised personnel access medical records.
  • Secure server storage and encrypted digital transmissions.
  • Periodic system audits and data recovery mechanisms.

5. Your rights under DPDPA 2023

You have specific rights regarding your personal information:

Right to access

Request details of the personal data held by us.

Right to correction

Request updates or corrections to inaccurate personal information.

Right to erasure

Request deletion of non-essential personal data, subject to medical record retention laws.

Right to withdraw consent

Withdraw consent for non-essential communications or data processing at any time by contacting our Data Protection Officer.

6. Cookies policy

Our website uses standard cookies to enhance the user browsing experience and analyse site traffic. You may choose to disable cookies in your browser settings, though certain interactive features may then function with limited capacity.
