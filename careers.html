Nursing

Wards, ICU, theatre, labour room and dialysis

  • Nursing Superintendent and ward in-charge posts for experienced candidates

  • Shift working, including nights, applies to most clinical nursing posts

Applying

How to apply

Apply online using the form below, or bring your documents to reception in person. Either way, call first to confirm the post is open.

Step by step

  • Check the vacancy list. See whether a post matching your qualification is currently open, and ring 04329 222530 to confirm before you apply. This saves an unnecessary journey.
  • Prepare your CV. One or two pages covering your qualification, registration number, previous hospitals and the shifts you are able to work.
  • Apply online, or in person. Complete the application form below, or hand your CV and copies of your certificates to reception during working hours.
  • Interview. If your application is taken forward, the hospital will contact you on the number you provide to arrange an interview and, where relevant, a practical assessment.
  • Verification. Original certificates and council registration are checked before any clinical appointment is confirmed.

What to bring

  • Your CV. One or two pages, with a mobile number the hospital can reach you on during the day.
  • Qualification certificates. Copies of your certificates and mark sheets. Bring the originals to interview, as they are checked before appointment.
  • Council registration certificate. Nursing, Medical or Pharmacy Council, as applicable to the post you are applying for.
  • Experience letters. Relieving or experience letters from previous employers, if you have them.
  • Photo ID and photographs. Any government photo ID, along with recent passport-size photographs.
  • Driving licence. A valid licence, for ambulance driver posts only.

Before you travel

Reception hours and the hospital's current recruitment needs change from time to time. A phone call before you set out is the quickest way to confirm both.
