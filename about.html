Who We Are

Trusted multi-speciality care for Ariyalur

A hospital built so that emergency medicine, surgery, critical care and advanced imaging are available in one place — without long-distance travel.

Hospital Overview

Complete care under one roof

Ariyalur Golden Hospital (PVT) Ltd. is a full-service multi-speciality healthcare centre committed to delivering timely, accessible and high-quality medical care to every patient who walks through our doors.

The accident and emergency department operates round the clock, supported by a 24-hour intensive care unit, an in-house blood bank and an ambulance service, so that critical patients receive attention without delay. Three ultra-modern operation theatres equipped with C-Arm support the full range of surgical work, and maternity services run 24 hours a day for deliveries and obstetric surgeries.

Our diagnostic wing offers MRI (1.5 Tesla), CT scan, digital X-ray and complete laboratory services, alongside a full endoscopy suite covering endoscopy, colonoscopy, colposcopy, hysteroscopy and laparoscopy. Additional services include poison treatment, dental and maxillofacial reconstruction, and physiotherapy rehabilitation.

Our Purpose

Mission, vision and the values we practise

The summaries below reflect the services published in our hospital brochure.

Our Mission

To make emergency, surgical and speciality care available to every family in Ariyalur district — promptly, affordably and with clinical honesty — so that no patient is forced to travel for treatment that can be delivered close to home.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted multi-speciality hospital in the region — recognised for round-the-clock emergency readiness, advanced diagnostics, ethical practice and compassionate treatment of every patient, whatever their means.

Compassion

Every patient is treated with dignity, patience and clear explanation in their own language.

Readiness

Emergency, theatre, imaging and blood bank stay available 24 hours a day, all year.

Integrity

Honest advice, transparent billing and full support with insurance and scheme paperwork.

Clinical Excellence

Specialist-led treatment supported by modern theatres, imaging and laboratory services.

Message from the Management

“Care should arrive before the ambulance does.”

Our purpose in establishing this hospital was simple: the people of Ariyalur deserve the same standard of emergency and speciality care available in the metros. That means an emergency room that never closes, a theatre that can be readied at any hour, imaging that produces answers on the same visit, and a billing desk that helps families use every scheme they are entitled to.

We continue to invest in equipment, in visiting super-speciality consultants, and most of all in the nursing and paramedical teams who are with the patient every hour of the stay.
