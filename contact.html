Appointments

Request an appointment by phone

Our reception team can help identify the appropriate department and check available consultation slots. For a medical emergency, call 99438 27233 immediately — do not use the appointment line.

Request an appointment

Call reception to enquire about outpatient appointments, scans and health check-ups. Keep these details ready when you call:

  • Patient name and age, and an existing patient ID if you have one
  • A callback number
  • The preferred department or doctor
  • Your preferred date
  • The main reason for the appointment. Do not use appointment booking for urgent medical advice

For a scan, ask whether a prescription, referral or preparation is required. For a health check-up, ask about package availability, fasting instructions and reporting time.

Insurance

Insurance enquiries

Call reception on 04329 222530 and ask for the insurance desk. Working hours are confirmed by reception.

Have these ready when you call

  • Patient name and a callback number
  • The insurer, TPA or government scheme name
  • Policy, scheme or beneficiary number
  • Whether the admission is planned or has already happened

The insurance desk can run a preliminary eligibility check and explain the documents required. Final coverage is decided by your insurer, TPA or government scheme. See Insurance & cashless treatment for details.

Protect your personal information

Do not send identity cards, policy documents or medical records unless a hospital representative provides an approved secure method. For a medical emergency, call 99438 27233 — do not delay urgent care while checking insurance eligibility.

Round-the-Clock Services

Services intended to operate 24/7

Availability of each service is confirmed by the hospital. Please call the Emergency Department on 99438 27233 before travelling for a specific service.

  • Emergency and accident assessment
  • Trauma assessment and initial management
  • Intensive care services, subject to clinical assessment and bed availability
  • Emergency assessment and initial management of suspected heart attack
  • Emergency assessment for suspected poisoning
  • Labour and delivery services — call the maternity team to confirm coverage
  • Ambulance enquiries on 99438 27233; availability and charges may vary
