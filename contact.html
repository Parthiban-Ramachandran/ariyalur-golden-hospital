Book an appointment

Reception is the fastest way to reserve an outpatient slot, a scan or a health check-up. Please keep these details ready when you call:

  • Patient name and age
  • The department or consultant you need
  • Your preferred date
  • A brief description of the symptoms, or the scan or test required
